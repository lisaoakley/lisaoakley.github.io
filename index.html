I am currently a Quantitative Researcher at Proof Trading, Inc., working on problems related to volume prediction, trading metric development, and detecting and controlling information leakage in US equities trading.

I received my PhD from Northeastern University in August 2025, where I was advised by Professor Alina Oprea. In my PhD, I studied the way tools from formal verification and probabilistic programming including probabilistic model checking and weighted model counting for exact probabilistic inference could be applied to verification of cybersecurity problems and differential privacy. I also developed methods for using probabilistic programming for privacy attacks. In addition to my primary research goals, I also worked on implementing adversarially robust reinforcement learning for cybersecurity applications, and applying reasoning from differential privacy to real world information leakage applications including U.S. equities trading.
